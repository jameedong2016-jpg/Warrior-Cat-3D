Clan journal

  1. Create your cat above, then begin.

Move: WASD, arrow keys, or hold and drag on the ground. Act: E or Space, or tap the button near things. Eat your catch: F. Fight: X. Eat a herb: H. Shake out / wriggle free: R. Crouch: hold C to sneak (prey notice you much later), release to pounce. Give prey to a clanmate: V (boosts reputation). Turn the camera: Q / Z, or right-click and drag the mouse (left/right turns, up/down tilts), or the Look buttons; wheel zooms. Speak: press Enter, type, Enter. Trees: walk to one and press E, then W/S to climb (jumping from high hurts). Talk: click a cat, then type. Pause: Esc. Kits drink milk from their mother. Stay out of other clans' camps and off the monster road. Rain soaks you, so find a den.
